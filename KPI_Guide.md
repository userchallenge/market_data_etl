# Financial KPI Guide

## Traditional Companies

**Core Metrics**
- **Revenue** - Total sales/income
- **EBITDA** - Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization (operational profitability)
- **FCF** - Free Cash Flow = Operating Cash Flow - Capital Expenditures (cash generation)

**Valuation Ratios**
- **P/E Ratio** - Price / Earnings Per Share (market valuation vs earnings)
- **P/S Ratio** - Market Cap / Revenue (market valuation vs sales)

## Banks

**Key Ratios**
- **ROA** - Return on Assets = Net Income / Total Assets (efficiency using assets)
- **ROE** - Return on Equity = Net Income / Shareholders Equity (returns to shareholders)
- **NIM** - Net Interest Margin = (Interest Income - Interest Expense) / Total Assets (core banking profitability)
- **Capital Ratio** - Shareholders Equity / Total Assets (financial strength)

## Investment Companies

**Key Ratios**
- **ROE** - Return on Equity = Net Income / Shareholders Equity (bottom-line profitability)
- **Investment Return** - Total Revenue / Total Assets (portfolio performance)
- **Net Margin** - Net Income / Total Revenue (operational efficiency)

## Crypto/Tech

**Core Metrics**
- **Revenue** - Total sales growth
- **EBITDA** - Operating profitability
- **FCF** - Cash generation capability

---

*All metrics include latest values, historical data (by year), and median growth rates for trend analysis.*
